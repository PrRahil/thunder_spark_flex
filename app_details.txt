1. Purpose of Our App
   We are grabbing the best delivery blocks for you — tailored perfectly for your preferences.

   1. Maximize earnings
   2. Save time
   3. Drive smarter

2. Flow of the App

   1. Login
      Connect your preferred delivery service directly within the app.

   2. Filters
      Tailor your search based on your ideal:

      * Dates
      * Times
      * Locations
      * Wages (optional / future scope)

   3. Start
      Once the user clicks the Start button, our system begins searching for delivery blocks in the background.

      We continuously refresh the APIs that provide available delivery blocks, filter them based on user preferences, and automatically grab the best matching blocks for the user.

      * Main Key Point:
        We charge a small fee for every successfully grabbed block (example: $9), so we also need to implement a wallet system within the app.

3. Smart AI Tapper

   We are building advanced AI-powered technology that constantly refreshes the delivery app and grabs the best suitable blocks for users within milliseconds (~50ms target speed).

   Since captchas may appear during the process, we also need to focus on handling them properly because they can sometimes become frustrating for users.

   Current Focus:
   Right now, we are focusing only on Amazon Flex as the primary service provider.

   FAQ — What is Amazon Flex?

   Amazon Flex is a delivery platform used by delivery partners across:

   * United States
   * Australia
   * Other countries

   The main purpose of the platform is to show available delivery slots/blocks that contain:

   * Wages (example: $250)
   * Time duration required to complete the deliveries
   * Warehouse pickup location
   * Delivery routes/orders

   Drivers must complete the assigned deliveries within the specified time duration and, in return, they earn a good amount of money.

4. User ScenariO

   Let’s create a hypothetical example:

   User A — Rahil wants to join our platform.

   First, Rahil needs to log in using his delivery service provider credentials such as:

   * Amazon Flex
   * Uber
   * Zomato
   * Other delivery platforms

   Therefore, we also need to implement secure authentication systems for each provider.

   After login, Rahil can configure filters such as:

   * Preferred locations
   * Preferred times
   * Desired wages
   * Preferred dates

   (Additional filters can be added in the future.)

   Once Rahil clicks the Start button:

   * If he does not have sufficient balance in his wallet, we will show an error message:

     “You do not have enough balance. Please recharge your wallet.”

   * If he has sufficient balance, our system will start searching for blocks in the background and find the best suitable opportunities for him.

   The system will automatically assign/grab the best matching block for Rahil.

   Important Note:
   If the block payment is $250, we do NOT deduct our charges from the driver's earnings.
   The service provider app pays the full amount directly to the user.

   Our platform charges its own separate service fee.

   Refund System:
   If the user cancels the grabbed block, the charged amount should be refunded back to the user's wallet.
